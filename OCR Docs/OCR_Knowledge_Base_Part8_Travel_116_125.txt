OCR Knowledge Base - Part 8 (Travel Documents 116-125)

============================================================ 116.
Boarding Pass
============================================================ Category:
Air Travel

Required Keywords: - boarding pass

Optional Keywords: - flight - gate - boarding time - seat - PNR -
passenger - terminal - zone - barcode - QR code - departure - arrival

Strong Indicators: - boarding pass - gate - seat - boarding time

Weak Indicators: - terminal - zone

Negative Keywords: - flight ticket

Regex / Identifier Guidance: - Airline PNR: typically 6 alphanumeric
characters

Common OCR Mistakes: - boardlng - fl1ght - terrninal

Classification Notes: Issued after check-in and required for boarding.
