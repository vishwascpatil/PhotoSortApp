============================================================ 17. OCI
Card (Overseas Citizen of India)
============================================================ Category:
Government Identity / Immigration

Required Keywords: overseas citizen of india oci oci card

Optional Keywords: government of india ministry of home affairs life
long visa multiple entry registration number card number passport number
nationality foreign passport place of birth date of birth surname given
name sex citizenship issued on valid till signature photograph

Strong Indicators: overseas citizen of india oci card ministry of home
affairs

Weak Indicators: passport number nationality foreign passport
citizenship

Negative Keywords: passport visa application pan card aadhaar driving
licence

Regex: OCI card numbers vary by issuance. Passport numbers may appear:
A-Z][0-9]{7}ommon OCR Mistakes: overseas citizen of india oversea
citizen 0CI OCII ministry of horne affairs govemment of india

Classification Notes: An OCI card almost always contains “Overseas
Citizen of India”. If OCR finds OCI together with passport details and
nationality, confidence should be very high.

============================================================ 18. PIO
Card (Person of Indian Origin)
============================================================ Category:
Government Identity / Immigration

Required Keywords: person of indian origin pio pio card

Optional Keywords: government of india ministry of home affairs passport
number nationality country date of birth surname given name place of
birth issued on valid till registration number signature photograph

Strong Indicators: person of indian origin pio card

Weak Indicators: passport number nationality

Negative Keywords: oci passport aadhaar pan card

Regex: Passport Number A-Z][0-9]{7}ommon OCR Mistakes: person of lndian
origin plo pi0 govemment

Classification Notes: PIO cards are older documents and may still exist.
If both “Person of Indian Origin” and passport details appear, classify
as PIO.

============================================================ 19.
Resident Certificate
============================================================ Category:
Government Certificate

Required Keywords: resident certificate residence certificate

Optional Keywords: resident residential address permanent resident state
government district magistrate deputy commissioner tahsildar tehsildar
village taluk district state certificate number issued on address father
name mother name guardian applicant

Strong Indicators: resident certificate residence certificate permanent
resident

Weak Indicators: district address certificate number

Negative Keywords: domicile certificate income certificate caste
certificate ration card

Regex: Certificate numbers vary by state.

Common OCR Mistakes: resident certiflcate resldence certificate
perrnanent resident govemment

Classification Notes: Resident Certificates mainly certify current
residence. Unlike Domicile Certificates, they do not usually certify
permanent state domicile rights.

============================================================ 20. Family
ID Card ============================================================
Category: Government Identity

Required Keywords: family id family identity family identification
number

Optional Keywords: family member head of family family head family
details household household id member id family number government state
government address mobile number relationship gender date of birth
father name mother name spouse children

Strong Indicators: family id family identification number household id

Weak Indicators: family member head of family relationship

Negative Keywords: ration card aadhaar voter id

Regex: Family IDs are state-specific and have no single standard format.

Common OCR Mistakes: famlly id family ld househoId head of famlly
govemment

Classification Notes: These cards usually list multiple family members
in a table along with their relationship, gender, age or DOB, and a
household/family ID.
