OCR Knowledge Base - Part 4 (Medical Documents 56-70)

============================================================ 56. Medical
Prescription
============================================================ Category:
Healthcare

Required Keywords: prescription rx

Optional Keywords: doctor physician consultant hospital clinic patient
name age gender diagnosis complaints medicine tablet capsule syrup
dosage twice daily once daily before food after food follow up
registration number medical council

Strong Indicators: rx prescription dosage

Weak Indicators: tablet capsule follow up

Negative Keywords: medical bill lab report

Regex: Doctor registration numbers vary.

Common OCR Mistakes: prescriptlon tabiet capsuie diag nosis

Classification Notes: Usually includes doctor’s details, patient details
and medicine instructions.
