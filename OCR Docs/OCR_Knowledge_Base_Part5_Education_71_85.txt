OCR Knowledge Base - Part 5 (Education Documents 71-85)

============================================================ 71. School
ID Card ============================================================
Category: Education Identity

Required Keywords: school identity card id card

Optional Keywords: student name admission number roll number class
section dob academic year principal school logo

Strong Indicators: school admission number class

Weak Indicators: section academic year

Negative Keywords: college employee id

Classification Notes: Usually contains photo, school logo and
admission/class information.
